# Coupled scalar implementation notes

The assumed equations are `phi_tt-a^2*laplacian(phi)+m^2*phi+g*chi=0` and `chi_tt-laplacian(chi)+M^2*chi+g*phi=J_chi`. In the fixed preferred time their Hamiltonian density is the sum of the two positive kinetic and gradient terms plus `(m^2*phi^2+M^2*chi^2)/2+g*phi*chi`. The energy flux is `-a^2*phi_t*grad(phi)-chi_t*grad(chi)` and the local source work is `J_chi*chi_t`. This prescribed forcing does not provide an apparatus, source backreaction or a detector observable.

The mass matrix is positive semidefinite exactly when `m^2>=0,M^2>=0,g^2<=m^2*M^2`. At positive-mass saturation `g=sign*m*M` its potential is `(m*phi+sign*M*chi)^2/2`. At zero mass stability forces `g=0`. Saturation is noncoercive: homogeneous null modes obey `q_tt=0` and may grow linearly. Absence of a negative frequency squared is not a bound on every field amplitude. Negative kinetic, negative gradient and supercritical potential directions are distinct pathologies checked by their actual formulas.

For a Fourier mode, `C=[[a^2*k^2+m^2,g],[g,k^2+M^2]]`; its shifted matrix `C-k^2*I` is positive semidefinite in the stable regime. Thus `omega_+/-^2>=k^2`. The implementation obtains eigenvectors from a symmetric rotation and the smaller eigenvalue from `det(C)/lambda_plus` to avoid subtractive cancellation. It verifies trace, determinant, characteristic equation, eigenpairs and matrix reconstruction rather than accepting the closed eigenvalue formula alone. Ordinary boosts preserve positive frequency for nonzero-frequency modes with subluminal boost velocity; a zero-frequency homogeneous mode remains zero and is not called strictly positive.

The principal differential terms have speeds `a` and `1`. Lower-order mixing does not change those principal cones, but the support of a response can involve both fields. The matrix retarded mode operator is `theta(t)*sin(t*sqrt(C))/sqrt(C)`, understood by its entire series at zero eigenvalues. This expression is conditional on the complete classical continuum system. Their independently evaluated partial fractions are `(1/Pb-1/Pa)/delta` and `1/(delta*Pb^2)+(1/Pa-1/Pb)/delta^2`. At `k=0` the products reduce directly to `s^-4,s^-6`. For equal speeds in 1+1 dimensions the null-coordinate retarded rectangle convolution gives `H=(t^2-r^2)/8` and `K=(t^2-r^2)^2/128` on `r<=t`, with zero values outside and at nonpositive time. These nonsingular expressions are tested separately.
